Recently, I started thinking of another one and its been 2 days and I'm still thinking about it so I figured it's best if I at least write down, however briefly, my thoughts/notes for it so I can forever look back on it.
	
The story would be probably based on an older story I've written but with some additional technical details that sparked this entire post. 

The "details" for this are as follow:
>In this future/alternate reality AI never became AGI but instead did become really efficient. It became so efficient and effective about 90% of jobs were eliminated. This caused a global level outroar from most civilizations which lasted for several years if not decades. In those years many people struggled to survive after the economic system was basically a prison for most people. 
>
>--no clue how to follow up with next section--
>
>After that brief period of turmoil AI was eventually contained in a special way. It evolved from being a mostly computer-esque process and was designed into a more biological process that can link to computers. This AI replaced phones and computers for most people. Phones and computers were still methods to interact with the internet and software but this new form of software that contained AI in this special way made way for the Augmented Reality era in which the story takes place.
>
>Once this AR era emerged it became common for people to have an implant similar to Bone Anchored Hearing Aids. This augmentation allowed people to do all things phones and computers normally did but without the physical object being necessary. It gave the person immediate visuals of information that they wanted to see. This augmentation had a way of generating controlled hallucinations since the information was not actually projected onto their eyes but it did seem this way from the user's perspective. 
>
>These augmentations would normally cost immense amount of processing but a method was invented so that it could use the brain of the user as a way to compute all these things with little to no hinderance to the person. Trading and money were reinstated once this augmentation and humanity's recovery was in full swing. This invention of brain processing created a new job many people picked up.
>
>A person could give up any amount of time of their day or week so that their brain could be used for processing power. During this period of time the person would go in a kind of artificually induced coma and would "dream". This processing could then be used for running simulations and calculations to solve many society benifiting things such as eradicating many diseases, improving humanity's ability to space travel and becoming a spacefaring civilization, and many more things. During this coma a person's body would resort in a kind of nest where many others would also be left and cared for. First by other people but eventually automated since there were some _incidents_ as they were essentially defenseless bodies.

That's all I've come up with so far but that's basically what I've been thinking about for the last 2 days so this is a nice way to get it out of my head. Can't wait to find out it got scrapped and someone AI generated a dogshit book out of it.
