Occasionally when I'm walking through my city, I see a set of fences blocking off a small section of a side walk. Those fences are most likely there because for a while there was like a small crane or something there and they wanted it to not be touched by random people. 

But because this would be a boring and way too obvious conclusion I like to imagine they are containing invisible anomolies. Those things you read about in SCP articles or like the things from [STALKER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.T.A.L.K.E.R.). 

So next time you walk past some fencing without anything within it look out... or take a closer look O.o
